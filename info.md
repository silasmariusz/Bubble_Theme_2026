## Bubble Theme 2026

An energetic Bubble Card skin with glassy gradients, neon accents and optional backdrop scripts.

- **Multiple vibes**: switchable palettes covering neutral, synthwave and playful variants.
- **Bubble-aware layouts**: optimized spacing for dashboards that rely on Bubble Cards.
- **Companion assets**: optional `www/` helpers for view settings and popup backgrounds.
- **Mobile polish**: card-mod tweaks deliver a subview-style single column look on desktop.

### Requirements

- Home Assistant `2024.2` or newer.
- Card-mod for additional layout adjustments.

### Installation

1. Add the repository through HACS (`Theme` category) and reload themes.
2. Ensure `frontend:` references `!include_dir_merge_named themes` in `configuration.yaml`.
3. Pick **Bubble Theme 2026** from your profile settings.

Optional: copy the `www/` helpers to `/config/www/bubble_theme_2026/` and load them via `module` resources to enable advanced popups.
